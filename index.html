Secondary Mathematics • Theoretical vs. Experimental Probability

🔀 Random Experiment

A process where the outcome cannot be predicted with certainty, having more than one possible result.

📚 Sample Space (S)

The collection of all possible outcomes. The total number of outcomes is denoted as n(S).

🎯 Event (E) & Favourable

An event is a subset of S. Favourable outcomes satisfy the event condition, with total count n(E).

📊 Probability of event E
P(E) = No of Favourable Outcomes Number of outcomes
📈

Law of Large Numbers

As the number of trials increases, the experimental probability converges towards the theoretical probability.
